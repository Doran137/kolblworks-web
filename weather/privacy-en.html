Kolbl Works – Privacy policy

Publisher and contact

KW Weather for Trips is published by Petr Kölbl under the Kolbl Works brand. Privacy and support contact: info@kolblworks.cz. These terms take effect on 16 October 2026.

No account or advertising

The app requires no account and contains no advertising or analytics tracking. From version 1.1.34, MET forecasts are downloaded through the Kolbl Works cache on kolblworks.cz, hosted by Webglobe. Earlier versions download directly from MET. Saved place names are not sent to the server.

Location and network requests

When refreshing a forecast, the app sends the selected place coordinates to the Kolbl Works server and through it to MET Norway (api.met.no), with at most four decimal places. This may be a manually saved place or the phone’s current location. Current location is used only with Android permission during a refresh while the app is open; the app does not track movement in the background. Saved places work without location permission. Place names and time zones are looked up offline.

Weather stations and providers

Nearby aviation observations are requested from the Aviation Weather Center, NOAA/NWS in the United States (aviationweather.gov). The search area centre is rounded to 0.5 degrees. In Czechia, the app also uses CHMI open data (opendata.chmi.cz); observation requests contain the station identifier. AWC, CHMI and the Kolbl Works hosting service receive your IP address and request details when a connection is made. The hosting service may record these details in access logs. From version 1.1.34, MET receives the coordinates and our server IP address; we do not forward the phone IP address. Earlier versions send requests directly, exposing the phone IP address to MET. Transfers use HTTPS. MET Norway logs IP addresses and requested coordinates on servers in Oslo. Providers manage their own logs; clearing app data does not remove those logs. Provider information: https://www.met.no/en/About-us/privacy and https://www.weather.gov/privacy; CHMI: https://www.chmi.cz/.

Storage and deletion

Saved places, settings, the last location, forecasts and refresh history remain on your phone. Each place keeps the current forecast and at most three older forecasts. The auxiliary MET cache holds at most 32 entries and is replaced over time. Removing a saved place deletes its forecasts and history, but auxiliary cache entries may remain until replaced. All local data can be removed by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling it. Android cloud backup of app data is disabled. The shared server cache holds at most 128 forecasts for coordinates and is replaced over time. It does not associate them with user accounts or store IP addresses. Clearing phone data does not remove the shared cache or hosting logs.

Your controls

Automatic refresh can be disabled in the app settings; manual refresh remains available. Location permission can be revoked in Android settings. Offline, the app displays downloaded data with its actual age. For questions about this policy or requests concerning your data, contact info@kolblworks.cz. Policy updates will carry a new date.
