Privacy Policy — Survival Guide (Průvodce přežitím)

Effective: 4 November 2026

Summary

Survival Guide works offline, has no account, ads or analytics, and sends nothing to any server by itself. Location, points, tracks, photos, voice notes and notes stay on the phone until you share or export them yourself.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Location — navigation and return to a point, saved places, last position and track recording. A track is recorded only by a service with a visible notification that you start yourself; the app has no background location access. Location can be turned off in the app.
  • Camera — live preview for tools (sky, measuring, aiming, Morse by camera). The preview is not stored. Photos for points are taken by the system camera.
  • Photo location data — for a photo you pick yourself, the app reads where and when it was taken so it can attach it to a point.
  • Microphone — voice notes (stored on the phone) and receiving Morse by sound (processed in memory only, not stored).
  • Wi-Fi and nearby devices — a direct link to another phone (Wi-Fi Direct) to pass a message or a point. Discovery is not used to determine location. Because of this link the app also has the network (internet) permission; it uses no server.
  • Notifications, exact-time alarms, start after restart — timers and reminders you set ring at the set time and are restored after a phone restart. Without exact-alarm permission they ring with a small delay.
  • Foreground services and wake lock — track recording and the SOS signal (flashlight or sound) keep running while the phone is locked, with a visible notification, until you stop them.

The app does not request contacts, SMS, calls or access to all photos and files.

When data leaves the phone (only by your choice)

  • Phone-to-phone link — you start it yourself; the link is encrypted and both sides must confirm a matching code. Only message text and points (name, coordinates, note) are transferred.
  • Sharing location, a point or a track (GPX) — the system share sheet opens and you choose whom and how. Shared location contains coordinates and map links.
  • External maps — when opening in Mapy.cz or Google Maps, the chosen app receives the target coordinates (Mapy.cz also its name).
  • Export and backup — you save the backup (.srb), voice notes and other exports through the system dialog wherever you choose. The manual backup is not encrypted — keep it as a sensitive file.

Google Play Services for AR (ARCore)

Augmented-reality measuring uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), provided by Google. It may process data under the Google Privacy Policy and the ARCore requirements.

Backup and third parties

Data is not included in Google cloud backup and is not transferred when moving to a new phone. The app contains no ads, analytics or crash reporting to third parties.

Deleting data

Delete individual points, tracks and records in the app. Delete all data by uninstalling the app or via Settings → Apps → Survival Guide → Storage → Clear data. Files you exported or shared stay where you sent them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
