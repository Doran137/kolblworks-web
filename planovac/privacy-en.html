Privacy Policy — Planner (Plánovač)

Effective: 4 November 2026

Summary

Planner has no internet access, collects no data and sends nothing anywhere on its own. Everything you enter (tasks, events, ideas, jobs, companies, people, photos and attachments) stays on your phone only. Data leaves the phone only when you send or copy it yourself, as described below.

Where data is stored

In the app's storage on your phone. Data is not included in Google cloud backup and is not transferred when moving to a new phone. For your own backup the app saves an export.json file and attachments in its folder Android/data/cz.kolblworks.planovac/files. Other apps cannot see it, but you can copy it to your own computer over a USB cable. Uninstalling the app deletes the data.

Permissions and why the app needs them

  • Notifications — reminders for tasks, events and drinking are shown as notifications.
  • Alarms at an exact time — so a reminder arrives at the exact time you set, not tens of minutes later.
  • Start after phone restart — after a restart, scheduled reminders are set again; otherwise they would be lost.
  • Vibration — a reminder notification vibrates.

The app does not ask for access to the internet, location, contacts, microphone or your gallery.

Photos and attachments

You take a photo with the system camera or pick one in the system picker; you pick an attachment in the system file picker. The app receives only the photo or file you choose and keeps its own copy.

When data leaves the phone (only by your choice)

  • Share / Send — the text of a task or overview opens in the system share sheet and you choose whom and how to send it to.
  • Tidy up with Claude — selected notes are handed, on your command, to the Claude app (Anthropic) on your phone. You choose which notes (e.g. today); the text appears in the message field of the Claude app and is sent only when you tap send. Further processing is governed by the Claude service terms. You paste the answer back from the clipboard and changes are shown in a preview before saving.
  • Address, phone, e-mail, link — tapping opens maps, the dialer, e-mail or the browser; the item is passed only to the app you open this way.
  • Copy to a computer — you can copy export.json and attachments over a cable (see above).

Ads, analytics, third parties

The app contains no ads, analytics or crash reporting to third parties. We have no access to your data.

Deleting data

Delete individual records in the app. Delete all data by uninstalling the app or via phone Settings → Apps → Planner → Storage → Clear data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
