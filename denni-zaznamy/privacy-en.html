Privacy Policy — Daily Records (Denní záznamy)

Effective: 5 November 2026 (version 0.9.2)

Summary

Daily Records has no internet access, no account, ads or analytics, and sends nothing by itself. You record health data in it, and that data stays only on your phone until you save it elsewhere or share it yourself.

What data the app records

  • Daily records: meals and their time, carbohydrates and other nutrients, blood glucose (fasting, before and after meals) and measurement time, insulin and its time, notes.
  • Drinks (kind, amount, carbohydrates).
  • Meal photos (up to 10 per record).
  • Settings: language, profile, reminder times, own foods and recipes.

Everything is stored in the app's private storage on the phone. Data is not included in Google cloud backup and is not transferred when moving to a new phone.

Photos

You take a photo with the system camera or pick one in the system photo picker. The app receives only the photo you choose, keeps a reduced copy, and has no camera or gallery permission.

Permissions

  • Notifications — reminders to measure, eat and take insulin. The notification text is generic and shows no measured values.
  • Exact reminder time (exact alarms) — so a reminder arrives at the set time.
  • Start after restart — after a phone restart, time change or app update, reminders are set again.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or file access.

When data leaves the phone (only by your choice)

  • Share diary — you first see the period and the exact text (CSV) that will be sent; you choose the app only after confirming. Notes are included only if you tick “Include notes”. The shared text contains health data — consider whom you send it to.
  • Export and backup — CSV and the backup (JSON) are saved through the system dialog wherever you choose. The backup is not encrypted and contains health data; photos are not included.
  • Print / PDF — through system printing.
  • Links to sources (e.g. diabetes guidance, food tables) open in your browser; the app itself downloads nothing.

Ads, analytics, third parties

The app contains no ads, analytics or crash reporting to third parties. The operator has no access to your data.

Deleting data

Delete individual records in the app; a record’s photos are deleted with it immediately. Delete all data including photos by uninstalling the app or via phone Settings → Apps → Daily Records → Storage → Clear data. Exports and backups you saved elsewhere stay there.

Health notice

The app is an aid for your own records; it does not replace a doctor or a medical device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
