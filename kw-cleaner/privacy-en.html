Privacy Policy — KW Cleaner

Effective: 4 November 2026

Summary

KW Cleaner has no internet access, collects no data and sends nothing anywhere. Storage analysis runs only on your phone.

What the app does with data

  • A folder you choose yourself — through the system folder picker the app reads the files in it, finds large files and exact duplicates and shows previews. Access to the folder lasts only until the app is closed. The storage root, the Android folder and cloud storage cannot be selected.
  • In this version the app only reads your files; deleting personal files is blocked.
  • Test files — the app can create artificial files in its private storage and delete them after you select and confirm.
  • A record of the cleaning progress is kept only in the app's private storage.

Permissions

  • Usage access — used only so the app can show how much space the cache of other apps takes. You grant it yourself in Android settings and can revoke it there at any time. The app does not read when or how long you use apps.
  • The list of installed apps is read only to show their names next to the cache size and is not sent anywhere.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera or microphone access and has no blanket access to all files.

Backup and third parties

App data is not included in cloud backup or in transfer to a new phone. The app contains no ads, analytics or crash reporting to third parties.

Deleting data

Delete all app data by uninstalling the app or via Settings → Apps → KW Cleaner → Storage → Clear data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
