Privacy Policy — Obrys

Effective: 4 November 2026

Summary

Obrys has no internet access, collects no data and sends nothing anywhere on its own. Photos, measurements and drawings stay on the phone until you save them elsewhere yourself.

What the app does with data

  • Photos of parts — taken with the system camera. The app itself has no camera permission; it receives the photo from the camera and stores it in its private storage.
  • Projects, measurements and calculations (outline, dimensions, drawing) are stored and computed only in the app's private storage.
  • Saving and opening a project or drawing — in the system dialog you choose where to save a file or which one to open. Where you save it (for example to the cloud) is your choice.

Permissions

The app requests no sensitive permissions: no internet, camera, location, contacts, microphone, or access to photos and files.

Backup and third parties

App data is not included in cloud backup or in transfer to a new phone. The app contains no ads, analytics or crash reporting to third parties.

Deleting data

Delete all app data by uninstalling the app or via Settings → Apps → Obrys → Storage → Clear data. Files you saved outside the app stay where you saved them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
