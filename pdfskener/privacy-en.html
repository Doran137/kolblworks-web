Privacy Policy — PDF Scanner

Effective from: 24 September 2026

Summary

The PDF Scanner app itself does not collect any personal data and never uploads your scanned documents. It stores them only on your device. The Google Play services scanning module used by the app may send Google technical data about the device and the scanning process (not the content of documents) — see “Google Play services and ML Kit” below. All processing — scanning, image adjustments and PDF creation — takes place entirely on your device.

What data the app processes

  • Photos of documents that you take or pick from the gallery. A saved document is always kept in the app’s private storage (the source of the list in the app) and, after a successful export, also in the Documents/PDF Scanner folder, which other apps can see. If the copy in the Documents folder cannot be created, the app tells you and the document stays only in the app until you repeat the export. Both copies exist only on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It requires no registration or sign-in.
  • It contains no analytics of its own, no ads and no tracking technologies. The only diagnostics that leave the device are the technical metrics of the Google scanning module (see below).
  • It does not send scanned documents, their images or their text to any server.

Permissions

  • Camera — used by the Google Play services scanning screen (ML Kit Document Scanner); the app itself does not request the camera permission and has no access to the camera outside scanning.
  • Images are accessed through the system file picker; the app has no blanket access to your gallery.
  • Internet access — used by Google Play services: they download the scanning module and its user interface before first use and may later contact Google servers for module updates and to send SDK technical metrics (see below). Without Google Play services the app cannot scan. The app itself opens no network connections of its own and documents are never uploaded.

Backup

System backup of the app is turned off: scanned documents are not copied to your Google account, not even when moving to a new phone. They stay on the device until you transfer or share them yourself.

Google Play services and ML Kit

To detect the edges of a document the app uses Google ML Kit Document Scanner. Detection runs directly on the device; images are not sent to Google. Google Play services download the scanning module and its interface dynamically before first use. Under the ML Kit terms these services may contact Google servers for updates and send technical data about SDK performance and usage (device information, app version and the scanning process, not the content of documents). This data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Your control over your data

Deleting a document in the app removes both copies — the private one and the one in the Documents/PDF Scanner folder. If the copy in the Documents folder cannot be deleted (for example a file created by an earlier installation of the app, which Android does not allow a new installation to manage), the app tells you and the file can be removed in the file manager. Uninstalling the app removes its private storage; the Documents/PDF Scanner folder stays until you delete it yourself.
